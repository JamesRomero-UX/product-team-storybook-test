# Voice & tone

Clear, trustworthy and calm. Write in plain language and **lead with the action**.

## Principles
- **Clear over clever** — say exactly what will happen.
- **Action-led** — buttons start with a verb: "Add risk".
- **Sentence case** — titles, labels, buttons, menus.
- **Honest in failure** — explain what happened and how to fix it.
- **Concise** — if a word can go without losing meaning, cut it.

## In practice
- ✅ "Couldn't save your changes. Check your connection and try again."
- ❌ "Oops! Something went wrong, please try later."
- ✅ Button: **Add control**
- ❌ Button: **Submit**
