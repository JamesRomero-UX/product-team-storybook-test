# Visual content

Photography, product imagery and data visualisation — used with purpose, never decoration.

## Photography
- Real, human, considered — credible people and workplaces, not stock clichés.
- Calm and confident; **avoid fear-based or alarmist imagery** in a risk context.
- Cool, neutral grades that sit alongside navy and teal.

## Product imagery
- Show the real product UI; keep dashboards legible and on-brand (teal accents, navy chrome).
- Frame screenshots in soft browser/device chrome; never stretch, skew, or add heavy shadows.

## Data visualisation
- **Teal** for the primary series, **navy** for structure; the rating palette carries status only.
- Heatmaps use the rating scale (Minimal → Critical); donuts/bars use teal + neutrals.
- Always label series and axes — never rely on colour alone.
