# Logo (masterbrand)

The RiskSmart wordmark is the primary brand identifier. Always place the supplied **master
artwork** — never retype or rebuild it in live text.

## Do
- Keep clear space around the wordmark equal to the height of the capital "R".
- Use navy/teal on light surfaces, white/teal on navy.
- Always use the supplied master artwork.

## Don't
- Recolour outside the approved navy, white and teal combinations.
- Add a space, hyphen or line break between "Risk" and "Smart".
- Stretch, skew, rotate, or add shadows or gradients.
