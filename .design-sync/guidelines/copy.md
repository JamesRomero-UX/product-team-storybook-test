# Copy

How RiskSmart writes — clear, trustworthy, consistent.

## Writing principles
Clear over clever · action-led (buttons start with a verb) · plain language · honest in failure ·
concise.

## Style guide
- **Sentence case everywhere** — titles, labels, buttons, menu items, headings.
- **Module/entity nouns are proper nouns** — Risk, Control, Action, Issue, Policy, Indicator stay
  capitalised mid-sentence.
- **Abbreviations keep their case** — CSV, ID, API.

### Capitalisation
- ✅ "Add new Risk", "Save changes", "Export to CSV", "Risk owner"
- ❌ "Add New Risk", "Save Changes", "Export To CSV", "Risk Owner"

### Numbers, dates & time
- Column labels avoid "At" — use **Created** / **Updated**, not "Created At".
- Dates as **"12 Jun 2026"**; sentence-case headers ("Due date", not "Due Date").
- Entity names stay capitalised in labels: "Risk title", "Control type".
